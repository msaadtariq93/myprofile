MBA Program

Executive MBA and MBA

The MBA program at GenevaTech Business School (GTBS) is a master’s program for experienced professionals, executives, entrepreneurs, and public sector leaders who aspire to become transformational leaders in their respective industries in an increasingly digital and technology-driven world.

Artificial Intelligence, data-informed management, sustainability, digital transformation, innovation, and ethical leadership are embedded across every program, ensuring graduates are equipped to lead organizations through change, create measurable impact, and thrive in an increasingly interconnected world.

Distinct from conventional MBA programs, the GTBS Professional MBA acknowledges prior professional experience and enables students to integrate their practical expertise into their academic learning, enhancing the value of their MBA degree.

The program combines Swiss-quality business education, technology-enabled learning, Recognition of Prior Professional Learning (RPL), and industry-focused specialization to create flexible, career-oriented MBA pathways across emerging disciplines. Through a structured RPL framework, participants may earn academic credit for validated professional experience, allowing them to focus on developing advanced managerial competencies and specialization.

Built on Swiss academic excellence and aligned with the European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System (ECTS), each Professional MBA combines strategic business education with industry-specific specialization, enabling participants to strengthen their leadership capabilities while addressing the evolving challenges of a technology-driven global economy.

The Executive MBA comprises 60 ECTS, which can be upgraded to Regular MBA (90 ECTS) by adding an applied research thesis. The program is delivered through flexible online and hybrid learning formats. The curriculum combines a common executive management core, discipline-specific specialization, rigorous RPL assessment, and an applied business transformation thesis that addresses a real organizational challenge.

The program develops strategic leaders who are capable of driving innovation, managing digital transformation, making evidence-based decisions, and creating sustainable value for organizations operating in today’s global knowledge economy.

Admission Criteria

The MBA program is aimed at graduates and professionals who want to develop leadership and business skills. GTBS follows a holistic admission process that considers academic merit, professional experience, leadership potential, and motivation to succeed in a globally competitive business environment.

Recognition of Prior Professional Learning (RPL)

A distinctive feature of the GTBS Executive Professional MBA is its RPL framework.

The RPL framework is a distinctive feature of our MBA Program, acknowledging that experienced professionals bring valuable knowledge, skills, and competencies acquired through their careers. Through a rigorous assessment of professional portfolios, leadership achievements, reflective practice, competency mapping, and, where appropriate, a professional interview, participants may be awarded corresponding number of ECTS for exhibited and validated professional learning. This approach recognizes workplace expertise as an integral part of lifelong learning, enabling participants to accelerate their academic journey while maintaining the highest standards of academic quality and professional integrity. Assessment may include:

  • Professional Portfolio
  • Career Achievement Profile
  • Reflective Learning Statement
  • Competency Mapping
  • Documentary Evidence
  • Professional Interview (Viva Voce)

This enables participants to convert validated workplace learning into academic credit while maintaining rigorous master’s-level standards.

Applied Capstone Project

The capstone project has become an integral part of the university degree curriculum. It can take various forms, but its purpose remains the same. The capstone project is a unique opportunity to carry out independent group research in order to devise an innovative solution for a real-world problem. While a project of this scope and scale can be challenging, it can also be very rewarding.

The capstone project is usually the final assignment and plays a vital role in preparing students for the world of work thanks to its practical applications and ability to help hone students’ professional knowledge and skills. Each capstone project report / professional thesis integrates academic knowledge with professional practice to deliver measurable organizational impact.

Worth 10 ECTS, the project requires learners to investigate a strategic issue either within their chosen specialization or in an inter-disciplinary business environment and develop practical, evidence-based solutions that deliver measurable organizational impact.

Whether undertaken as an applied research study, consultancy assignment, innovation strategy project, policy analysis, teaching practice or business improvement plan or digital transformation initiative, the thesis demonstrates the participant’s ability to integrate leadership, strategic thinking, innovation, and analytical skills to address complex business and societal challenges.

The European Standard

The European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System (ECTS) is the standard academic credit framework used by universities and higher education institutions across the European Higher Education Area (EHEA). It provides a transparent and internationally recognized method for measuring the volume of learning based on clearly defined learning outcomes and student workload. By standardizing credits, ECTS makes it easier for students to transfer between institutions, compare qualifications, and pursue further education or professional opportunities across Europe.

The European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System itself does not prescribe the duration of a degree. Instead, the Bologna Process has established widely accepted ranges for higher education qualifications across the European Higher Education Area. While there are national variations, the following ranges are the most commonly accepted.

Under the ECTS framework, one academic year of full-time study normally corresponds to 60 ECTS credits, representing approximately 1,500–1,800 hours of total student learning. This includes not only classroom instruction, but also independent study, assignments, projects, assessments, practical activities, and research. As a result, ECTS credits provide a unified measure that supports the comparability of academic qualifications across different countries and institutions within the European Higher Education Area.

The GenevaTech PQF

The Professional Qualifications Framework (PQF) of GenevaTech Business School is based on the ECTS framework. It spells out the institutional qualifications architecture spanning technology, business, education, AI, finance, cybersecurity, and entrepreneurship.

ECTS credits facilitate academic transparency, support lifelong learning, and make it easier for learners to demonstrate the volume and level of their studies when pursuing further education or professional development. While the recognition of qualifications is always subject to the policies of individual institutions, employers, and national regulatory authorities, the use of the ECTS framework enhances the international comparability and portability of academic achievements.

To ensure that 1 ECTS is academically defensible under the European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System (ECTS), each module explicitly corresponds with the total student workload, not just contact hours. According to the ECTS Users’ Guide, 1 ECTS corresponds to approximately 25–30 hours of learner workload. The GTBS adopts 30 hours per ECTS as it aligns well with executive / professional education. The PQF awards 1 ECTS to any short course / activity as long as it corresponds to the following workload:

Learning ActivityHours
Live Interactive Lectures8
Guided Reading & Self-Study6
Practical Laboratory / Software Exercises6

This standardized template applies uniformly to every professional certificate, diploma, and degree program regardless of discipline—finance, AI, blockchain, education, cybersecurity, entrepreneurship, or sustainability. It simplifies curriculum design, demonstrates transparent alignment with ECTS workload expectations, and provides a robust foundation for internal quality assurance and future external review.
