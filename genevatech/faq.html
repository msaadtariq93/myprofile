Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about GTBS, our programs, credits and how to apply.

01What is GenevaTech Business School (GTBS)?

GTBS is a Swiss business school based in Geneva, offering technology-enabled executive education and higher education programs designed to prepare future-ready leaders for the digital economy.

02What makes GTBS unique?

GTBS combines Swiss-quality education with AI-integrated learning, industry-focused curriculum, flexible study options, and ECTS-aligned stackable qualifications.

03What programs do you offer?

We offer Professional Certificates, Professional Diplomas, Executive Education, Bachelor's, Master's, Doctoral programmes, and customized corporate training.

Browse programs

04Who can apply?

Our programs are designed for professionals, entrepreneurs, executives, graduates, educators, and organizations seeking to upskill or reskill.

05How do I apply?

Simply complete the online application, upload the required documents, and our Admissions Team will guide you through the remaining steps.

Start your application

06How are courses delivered?

Programmes are offered online, on campus, and in hybrid formats, providing maximum flexibility for working professionals.

07What are ECTS and stackable qualifications?

ECTS (European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System) credits measure learning workload and achievement. GTBS allows learners to accumulate credits through stackable certificates that lead to higher qualifications.

How credits work

08How are participants assessed?

Assessment focuses on practical application through assignments, case studies, projects, presentations, and capstone activities rather than traditional examinations alone.

09What will I receive after successful completion?

Participants receive a Professional Certificate, ECTS credits (where applicable), Digital Badge and Competency Transcript.

10How will a GTBS qualification benefit my career?

Our programs develop practical, future-ready skills in business and emerging technologies, helping professionals advance their careers and lead digital transformation.

11Corporate Learning Solutions

Looking to upskill your workforce?

GTBS designs customized executive education and corporate training programmes tailored to your organization's needs in:

  • Artificial Intelligence (AI)
  • FinTech & Blockchain
  • Digital Transformation
  • Leadership & Management
  • Cybersecurity
  • Education Technology (EdTech)
  • Business Analytics
12Start Your Learning Journey

Whether you're looking to advance your career, acquire new digital skills, or earn internationally aligned professional qualifications, GTBS offers flexible learning pathways to help you achieve your goals.
