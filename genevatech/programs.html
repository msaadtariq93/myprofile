The European Standard

How credits work at GTBS

The European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System (ECTS) is the standard academic credit framework used by universities and higher education institutions across the European Higher Education Area (EHEA). Under the ECTS framework, one academic year of full-time study normally corresponds to 60 ECTS credits, representing approximately 1,500–1,800 hours of total student learning.

According to the ECTS Users’ Guide, 1 ECTS corresponds to approximately 25–30 hours of learner workload. The GTBS adopts 30 hours per ECTS as it aligns well with executive / professional education.
