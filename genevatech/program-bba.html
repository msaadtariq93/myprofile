BBA Program

Bachelor of Business Administration

The Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) at GenevaTech Business School (GTBS) is a 180 ECTS, 3-year undergraduate program designed to prepare future business leaders, entrepreneurs, and innovators for success in the digital economy.

Combining business fundamentals with emerging technologies, Artificial Intelligence, entrepreneurship, and sustainability, the programme equips students with the knowledge, practical skills, and global perspective required to thrive in an increasingly interconnected world. Through experiential learning, industry projects, internships, and international exposure, students develop the competencies to lead organizations, create new ventures, and drive responsible innovation.

Admission Criteria

Admission to the BBA is determined by academic merit, English language proficiency, and the applicant’s motivation. GTBS welcomes applications from students of diverse educational backgrounds who demonstrate the potential to succeed in a rigorous, internationally oriented learning environment.

Experienced applicants may receive academic recognition for demonstrated competencies through the RPL framework.

The European Standard

The European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System (ECTS) is the standard academic credit framework used by universities and higher education institutions across the European Higher Education Area (EHEA). It provides a transparent and internationally recognized method for measuring the volume of learning based on clearly defined learning outcomes and student workload. By standardizing credits, ECTS makes it easier for students to transfer between institutions, compare qualifications, and pursue further education or professional opportunities across Europe.

The European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System itself does not prescribe the duration of a degree. Instead, the Bologna Process has established widely accepted ranges for higher education qualifications across the European Higher Education Area. While there are national variations, the following ranges are the most commonly accepted.

Under the ECTS framework, one academic year of full-time study normally corresponds to 60 ECTS credits, representing approximately 1,500–1,800 hours of total student learning. This includes not only classroom instruction, but also independent study, assignments, projects, assessments, practical activities, and research. As a result, ECTS credits provide a unified measure that supports the comparability of academic qualifications across different countries and institutions within the European Higher Education Area.

The GenevaTech PQF

The Professional Qualifications Framework (PQF) of GenevaTech Business School is based on the ECTS framework. It spells out the institutional qualifications architecture spanning technology, business, education, AI, finance, cybersecurity, and entrepreneurship.

ECTS credits facilitate academic transparency, support lifelong learning, and make it easier for learners to demonstrate the volume and level of their studies when pursuing further education or professional development. While the recognition of qualifications is always subject to the policies of individual institutions, employers, and national regulatory authorities, the use of the ECTS framework enhances the international comparability and portability of academic achievements.

To ensure that 1 ECTS is academically defensible under the European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System (ECTS), each module explicitly corresponds with the total student workload, not just contact hours. According to the ECTS Users’ Guide, 1 ECTS corresponds to approximately 25–30 hours of learner workload. The GTBS adopts 30 hours per ECTS as it aligns well with executive / professional education. The PQF awards 1 ECTS to any short course / activity as long as it corresponds to the following workload:

This standardized template applies uniformly to every professional certificate, diploma, and degree program regardless of discipline—finance, AI, blockchain, education, cybersecurity, entrepreneurship, or sustainability. It simplifies curriculum design, demonstrates transparent alignment with ECTS workload expectations, and provides a robust foundation for internal quality assurance and future external review.

Specializations

The Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) at GenevaTech Business School offers a range of future-focused specializations that enable students to develop expertise in high-demand business disciplines while building a strong foundation in management, leadership, and digital innovation. Each specialization combines academic rigor with practical application, integrating emerging technologies, Artificial Intelligence, data-driven decision-making, sustainability, and global business perspectives.

Designed in consultation with industry trends and international best practices, these specializations prepare graduates for successful careers in the private, public, and entrepreneurial sectors, while providing a strong pathway to postgraduate studies and lifelong professional development. Additionally, the students benefit from exposure to real-world challenges and innovative learning experiences, equipping them with the adaptability and skills needed to thrive in a rapidly evolving global business landscape.

  • AI and Business Transformation
  • FinTech and Digital Finance
  • Business Analytics and Data Science

  • Entrepreneurship and Start-up Management

  • Education Technology and AI
