Program Overview

Finance, technology, innovation, ethics and sustainability in one diploma

As financial services continue to be transformed by Artificial Intelligence (AI), Financial Technology (FinTech), blockchain, digital assets, data analytics, and sustainable finance, organizations require professionals who possess both strong financial knowledge and the ability to apply emerging technologies strategically and responsibly.

Structured as a 12 ECTS Diploma, the program comprises six integrated modules, each carrying 2 ECTS and designed to progressively build participants’ knowledge and professional competencies. The program begins with Fundamentals of Stock Investing, introducing financial markets, investment principles, and portfolio management. It then explores the digital transformation of financial services through Fundamentals of FinTech, followed by Fundamentals of Blockchain Technology, which examines distributed ledger technologies, cryptocurrencies, smart contracts, and enterprise blockchain applications. The fourth module, Artificial Intelligence for Finance & Investment, focuses on AI-driven financial analysis, investment decision-making, and automation. Participants then study Sustainable Finance, ESG & Digital Assets, gaining insights into responsible investing, climate finance, and the evolving digital asset landscape. The program culminates in Digital Portfolio Management & Integrated Capstone, where participants integrate knowledge from all modules to develop a practical digital finance strategy for a real or simulated organization.

Delivered over 24 weeks through flexible online and hybrid learning modes, the program follows the GenevaTech Applied Learning Model, combining interactive teaching, guided self-study, practical laboratory exercises, industry case studies, collaborative learning, and project-based assessment. Throughout the program, participants develop a Professional Digital Portfolio that demonstrates their practical competencies and supports career advancement.

Aligned with the GenevaTech Professional Qualifications Framework and the European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System (ECTS), the diploma represents a total learner workload of 300 hours, ensuring transparency, academic rigour, and international recognition of learning outcomes. Upon successful completion of all six modules and the integrated capstone project, participants are awarded the Diploma in Digital Finance (12 ECTS).

The program is designed for banking and finance professionals, investment practitioners, entrepreneurs, regulators, business executives, and graduates seeking to strengthen their expertise in digital finance.

Integrated Capstone Project (see below).

Integrated Capstone Project

Participants work individually or in small teams to prepare a comprehensive Digital Finance Transformation Strategy for a real or simulated organization. The project should include:

  • Digital finance maturity assessment
  • FinTech adoption roadmap
  • Blockchain use-case evaluation
  • AI-enabled investment or operational analysis
  • ESG integration strategy
  • Risk assessment and regulatory considerations
  • Implementation roadmap (12–24 months)
  • Financial and business case
  • Executive presentation and defense before a faculty panel
