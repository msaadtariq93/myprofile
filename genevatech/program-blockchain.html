Program Overview

Building Trust, Transparency and Innovation in the Digital Economy

Blockchain technology has emerged as one of the most significant technological innovations of the twenty-first century, fundamentally changing the way organizations record, verify and exchange information. Originally developed to support digital currencies, blockchain has evolved into a transformative platform for banking, trade finance, supply chain management, healthcare, digital identity, government services and numerous other sectors requiring secure, transparent and decentralized record-keeping.

The Fundamentals of Blockchain Technology module has been developed by GenevaTech Business School (GTBS) as the third module of the Professional Diploma in Digital Finance. The course introduces participants to the principles of distributed ledger technology, cryptographic security, consensus mechanisms, smart contracts and tokenization, while examining practical business applications across multiple industries. Particular emphasis is placed on understanding blockchain from a strategic and managerial perspective rather than a software engineering perspective.

Delivered through the GenevaTech Applied Learning Model (GALM™), the module combines interactive lectures, practical demonstrations, AI-supported learning activities, industry case studies and applied exercises. Participants will gain the confidence to evaluate blockchain opportunities, understand emerging business models and make informed decisions regarding blockchain adoption within their organizations.

Led by veteran investor Dr. Shoaib Zafar, CFA, CAIA, this is a 4-week hybrid course.

Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this module, participants will be able to:

  • Explain the principles and architecture of blockchain technology.
  • Differentiate between public, private and consortium blockchain networks.
  • Describe consensus mechanisms and their role in securing decentralized systems.
  • Evaluate the business value of smart contracts and decentralized applications.
  • Understand cryptocurrencies, stablecoins, tokenization and digital assets.
  • Assess enterprise blockchain use cases across multiple industries.
  • Analyse regulatory, governance and cybersecurity considerations relating to blockchain adoption.
  • Design a blockchain-based business solution for a real-world organizational challenge.

Weekly Breakdown

Every week combines a 2 hour live session, 3 hours of guided self-study and a practical laboratory.

Week 1Blockchain Fundamentals and Distributed Ledger

Live Session (2 Hours)

  • Evolution of blockchain technology
  • Distributed ledger concepts
  • Blockchain architecture
  • Blocks, hashing and cryptographic principles
  • Public, private and consortium blockchains
  • Blockchain ecosystems and terminology

Self-Study & Independent Learning (3 Hours)

  • Explore the evolution of blockchain from Bitcoin to enterprise platforms.
  • Compare centralized and decentralized systems.
  • Read introductory blockchain reports.
  • Prepare a reflection on the role of trust in digital transactions.

Practical Laboratory

  • Blockchain Explorer
  • Ethereum Explorer
  • ChatGPT for blockchain research
  • Interactive blockchain visualizations
Week 2Consensus Mechanisms, Smart Contracts and Decentralized Applications

Live Session (2 Hours)

  • Proof of Work (PoW)
  • Proof of Stake (PoS)
  • Delegated consensus models
  • Smart contracts
  • Ethereum ecosystem
  • Decentralized Applications (DApps)
  • Web3 overview

Self-Study & Practical Exercise (3 Hours)

  • Compare major consensus mechanisms.
  • Analyse a smart contract use case.
  • Evaluate a decentralized application.

Practical Laboratory

  • MetaMask Wallet (demonstration)
  • Ethereum Test Network
  • Smart Contract Explorer
  • AI-assisted smart contract analysis
Week 3Digital Assets, Tokenization and Enterprise Blockchain

Live Session (2 Hours)

  • Cryptocurrencies and stablecoins
  • Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs)
  • Tokenization of real-world assets
  • Enterprise blockchain platforms
  • Supply chain applications
  • Trade finance
  • Healthcare and digital identity

Self-Study & Project (3 Hours)

  • Evaluate an enterprise blockchain implementation.
  • Compare tokenization models.
  • Analyse a CBDC initiative.

Practical Laboratory

  • Token Explorer
  • Digital Asset Dashboards
  • Blockchain Demo Platforms
  • AI-assisted business model analysis
Week 4Blockchain Strategy, Governance and Future Trends

Live Session (2 Hours)

  • Blockchain governance
  • Regulatory developments
  • Cybersecurity and privacy
  • Blockchain adoption strategies
  • Emerging trends
  • Participant project presentations

Self-Study & Capstone (3 Hours)

  • Develop a Blockchain Business Transformation Proposal including: business problem, proposed blockchain solution, technology architecture, stakeholder analysis, benefits and risks, governance considerations, implementation roadmap.

Practical Laboratory

  • Participant project presentations

Industry Case Studies

Participants will analyse:

  • Ethereum – Smart Contract Innovation
  • Ripple – Cross-Border Payments
  • IBM Food Trust – Supply Chain Transparency
  • JPM Coin – Institutional Digital Payments
  • TradeLens – Global Trade Documentation
  • Swiss Digital Exchange (SDX) – Tokenized Financial Markets
  • Project mBridge – Cross-Border Central Bank Digital Currency Collaboration

Each case examines business value, governance, implementation challenges, regulatory implications and lessons learned.
