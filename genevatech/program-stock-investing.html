Program Overview

From financial literacy to active market execution

The global investment landscape is becoming increasingly sophisticated, data-driven and technology-enabled. Today’s investors need far more than a basic understanding of stocks and financial statements. They must interpret macroeconomic developments, analyse businesses, evaluate risks, use AI-powered analytical tools and construct diversified portfolios aligned with long-term financial objectives.

Fundamentals of Stock Investing is the foundation course of the Professional Diploma in Digital Finance. Combining investment theory with practical application, it introduces the principles of equity investing alongside the analytical methods, screening techniques and portfolio management practices used by professional investors and wealth managers.

Participants work with real financial data, institutional research resources, AI-assisted investment tools and portfolio construction techniques to develop their own investment strategy, through the GenevaTech Applied Learning Model.

Led by veteran investor Dr. Shoaib Zafar, CFA, CAIA, this 4-week hybrid course bridges the gap between financial literacy and active market execution. By the end of this program, you will possess the confidence and skills required to independently discover, evaluate, and manage a portfolio of public equities.

Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this module, participants will be able to:

  • Explain the structure and functioning of global capital markets.
  • Interpret macroeconomic indicators and their influence on equity markets.
  • Analyse financial statements and assess corporate financial performance.
  • Apply fundamental investment analysis to evaluate listed companies.
  • Use professional screening tools to identify investment opportunities.
  • Construct diversified portfolios using sound risk management principles.
  • Apply AI-enabled research tools to support investment analysis.
  • Develop and present a personal investment strategy based on evidence.

Weekly Breakdown

Every week combines a 2 hour live session, 3 hours of guided self-study and a practical laboratory.

Week 1Understanding Financial Markets & the Global Economy

Live Session (2 Hours)

  • Structure of the global financial system
  • Stock exchanges and capital markets
  • Economic cycles and market behaviour
  • Inflation, interest rates and monetary policy
  • Market indices and sector performance
  • Building a professional market dashboard

Self-Study & Independent Learning (3 Hours)

  • Analyse current global market conditions
  • Compare the policies of major central banks
  • Read selected market outlook reports
  • Prepare a personal investment objectives statement

Practical Laboratory

  • Yahoo Finance
  • TradingView
  • MarketWatch
  • Google Finance
  • ChatGPT for market summaries
Week 2Evaluating Businesses Through Fundamental Analysis

Live Session (2 Hours)

  • Reading financial statements
  • Revenue and profitability analysis
  • Cash flow assessment
  • Financial ratios
  • Business quality
  • Competitive advantage
  • Corporate governance

Self-Study & Practical Exercise (3 Hours)

  • Analyse the annual report of a listed company
  • Calculate key financial ratios
  • Prepare an investment summary

Practical Laboratory

  • Annual reports
  • Excel financial modelling
  • AI-assisted ratio interpretation
Week 3Finding High-Quality Investment Opportunities

Live Session (2 Hours)

  • Professional stock screening
  • Growth vs value investing
  • Quality investing
  • Economic moats
  • Valuation basics
  • Investment watchlists

Self-Study & Project (3 Hours)

  • Design a customized stock screening strategy
  • Build a personal investment watchlist
  • Select one company for investment analysis

Practical Laboratory

  • Finviz
  • Investing.com
  • AI-assisted company research
Week 4Portfolio Construction & Investment Decision-Making

Live Session (2 Hours)

  • Portfolio diversification
  • Position sizing

  • Behavioural finance
  • Long-term investment discipline
  • Presentation of stock recommendations

Self-Study & Capstone (3 Hours)

  • Investment philosophy
  • Asset allocation

Practical Laboratory
