Program Overview

Understanding the Digital Transformation of Financial Services

Financial Technology (FinTech) has transformed the global financial services industry, creating new business models, improving customer experiences, expanding financial inclusion, and reshaping the competitive landscape of banking and finance. Technologies such as cloud computing, mobile banking, digital payments, Artificial Intelligence (AI), Open Banking, Application Programming Interfaces (APIs), blockchain, and embedded finance are redefining how financial products and services are developed, delivered, and consumed.

The Fundamentals of FinTech module provides participants with a comprehensive introduction to the FinTech ecosystem and the technologies driving digital financial innovation. Designed by GenevaTech Business School (GTBS) as the second module of the Professional Diploma in Digital Finance, the course bridges traditional financial services with emerging digital technologies, enabling participants to understand how financial institutions, technology companies, regulators, and startups are transforming the future of finance.

The participants will explore practical applications of digital banking, payment systems, lending platforms, wealth management, regulatory technology (RegTech), InsurTech, and AI-driven financial services. Through interactive discussions, practical laboratories, industry case studies, and a hands-on capstone project, participants will gain the knowledge and skills required to evaluate and implement FinTech solutions within their own organizations.

Led by veteran investor Dr. Shoaib Zafar, CFA, CAIA, this is a 4-week hybrid course.

Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this module, participants will be able to:

  • Explain the evolution and structure of the global FinTech ecosystem.
  • Analyse how digital technologies are transforming financial services.
  • Evaluate digital banking, payment systems, and embedded finance models.
  • Understand Open Banking, APIs, and Banking-as-a-Service (BaaS).
  • Assess emerging FinTech sectors, including RegTech, InsurTech, WealthTech, and SupTech.
  • Apply AI-enabled tools to analyse FinTech business models.
  • Evaluate regulatory, cybersecurity, and ethical challenges in digital finance.
  • Develop a FinTech solution addressing a real-world business problem.

Weekly Breakdown

Every week combines a 2 hour live session, 3 hours of guided self-study and a practical laboratory.

Week 1The FinTech Revolution: Understanding the Digital Financial Ecosystem

Live Session (2 Hours)

  • Evolution of FinTech and digital disruption
  • Global FinTech landscape
  • FinTech business models
  • Digital transformation in banking
  • Emerging opportunities and challenges

Self-Study & Independent Learning (3 Hours)

  • Review the evolution of global FinTech.
  • Compare FinTech ecosystems in Switzerland, Europe, Asia, and North America.
  • Read selected reports on global FinTech trends.
  • Prepare a brief reflection on the future of financial services.

Practical Laboratory

  • CB Insights FinTech Market Map
  • Crunchbase
  • ChatGPT for FinTech research
  • Statista market reports
Week 2Digital Banking, Payments and Open Banking

Live Session (2 Hours)

  • Digital banking models
  • Challenger and neo banks
  • Mobile banking
  • Digital wallets
  • Open Banking
  • Banking-as-a-Service (BaaS)
  • APIs in financial services

Self-Study & Practical Exercise (3 Hours)

  • Compare the features of three leading digital banks.
  • Analyse a digital payment ecosystem.
  • Explore Open Banking use cases.

Practical Laboratory

  • Stripe Dashboard
  • Wise
  • Revolut
  • Plaid API demonstrations
Week 3FinTech Innovation and Emerging Technologies

Live Session (2 Hours)

  • AI in financial services
  • RegTech
  • InsurTech
  • WealthTech
  • Embedded finance
  • Digital lending
  • Financial inclusion

Self-Study & Project (3 Hours)

  • Select a FinTech company and analyse its business model.
  • Identify the technologies enabling its competitive advantage.
  • Prepare an innovation brief.

Practical Laboratory

  • Microsoft Copilot
  • Google Gemini
  • Figma (for customer journey mapping)
  • AI-assisted business model analysis
Week 4Designing Digital Financial Solutions

Live Session (2 Hours)

  • Designing customer-centric financial services
  • Digital transformation strategy
  • FinTech regulation and compliance
  • Cybersecurity considerations
  • Future trends in financial innovation
  • Participant project presentations

Self-Study & Capstone (3 Hours)

  • Develop a FinTech Business Solution for a selected organization, including: business challenge, customer needs, proposed digital solution, technology architecture, business model, implementation roadmap, risks and mitigation strategies.

Practical Laboratory

  • Participant project presentations

Practical Laboratory

Throughout the programme, participants will work with:

  • Stripe
  • Plaid (API demonstrations)
  • ChatGPT
  • Figma
  • Business Model Canvas
  • Financial APIs (demonstration level)

Industry Case Studies

Participants will analyse:

  • Stripe – Global Payments Innovation
  • Revolut – Digital Banking Transformation
  • Wise – Cross-Border Payments
  • Klarna – Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL)
  • Nubank – Digital Financial Inclusion
  • Adyen – Enterprise Payments
  • Monzo – Customer-Centric Digital Banking

Each case explores business strategy, customer experience, technology adoption, scalability, regulation, and competitive positioning.
