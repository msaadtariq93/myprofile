About Us

GENEVATECH Business School

GenevaTech Business School stands at the intersection of business and technology, offering a transformative learning experience rooted in Swiss excellence and recognized across Europe.

With a commitment to innovation and global impact, GenevaTech provides hybrid learning pathways that empower students to thrive in a rapidly changing digital landscape.

Our programs

Vision

To be Europe’s leading hub for technology-enabled business education.

Mission

Deliver future-focused, accessible business and technology education for the digital economy.

Our story

Why GENEVATECH Business School

  1. Cutting-edge curriculum integrating technology and business, including specialized degrees in Digital Business, Artificial Intelligence, FinTech, and Cybersecurity.

  2. Emphasis on practical, real-world skills and an entrepreneurial mindset to prepare students for dynamic career opportunities in the digital economy.

  3. Global outlook with a focus on innovation, digital leadership, and emerging technologies.

Academic Architecture

ECTS, RPL and hybrid learning, by design

All our programs follow the European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System (ECTS). Our Recognition of Prior Professional Learning (RPL) framework values relevant professional experience. Study through a flexible blend of classroom learning, live online sessions, online assessments, digital resources, and workplace-based projects.
